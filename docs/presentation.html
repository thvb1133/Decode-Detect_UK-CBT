The problem

Shape is not enough.

  • 40,131 labelled payments, 3.1% suspicious (1,250). Test set: 40,101 payments.
  • Motifs include fan-in, fan-out, chains, cycles, stacks, relays.
  • Every motif family contains both suspicious payments and legitimate look-alikes. Recognising the shape cannot separate them.
  • The metric is average precision: rank the suspicious payments first. 25 hard cases are labelled independently of any observable feature.

The method

Score the context, not the pattern.

  • One effective payment per transaction, valued in reference USD at the hour of the ledger.
  • Flow. Did a near-identical amount arrive just before, and leave just after?
  • Pair and velocity. Is this amount normal for this relationship, and is the account bursting right now?
  • Account and graph. Conduits, new edges, short cycles, shared neighbours.
  • LightGBM, 5 folds × 3 repeats. Test scores are the mean percentile rank across fold models.
